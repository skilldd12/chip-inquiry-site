01Can I still inquire about a part that is not in stock?
Yes. This site lists a sourcing range, not stock. Send the part number, quantity and target date code; we check availability with the channel and either quote or tell you clearly that it cannot be supplied.
02What BOM format do you accept?
CSV, XLS or XLSX. At minimum a part number and quantity column; brand, package and reference designators help. Files stay private and are used only for this inquiry and quotation.
03Are you an authorised distributor?
We do not claim any unconfirmed authorisation or distributorship. Brand names only indicate that sourcing requests are accepted. If you need authorised-chain documents, state it in the inquiry and we will answer with what can actually be provided.
04What is the minimum order quantity?
There is no single MOQ. It depends on the part, packaging and channel: full reel, tray and cut tape follow different rules. The quotation states the actual MOQ and packing unit for that part.
