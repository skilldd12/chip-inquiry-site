What we actually do

Services around the requirement

Single parts, multi-line BOMs, supply changes and order delivery each have a practical path.

01

Brand sourcing

Given a brand, part number and quantity, we check availability, date code and packaging with the relevant channel before quoting a workable price and lead time.

02

BOM kitting

Upload a BOM and we check part number, package and quantity per line, flagging shortages, end-of-life parts and lines with multiple acceptable sources.

03

Supply and alternatives

When the original part cannot be supplied, we propose alternatives with comparable parameters and package, stating the differences and risks for you to decide.

04

Delivery and documents

Orders are picked, verified, packed and shipped with usable packing information. Inspection reports or accompanying documents are arranged when specified in the inquiry.

05

Quality verification

Packaging, labels, markings and quantity are checked per date code. Any anomaly pauses shipment until we agree on how to handle it.

How a request is handled

Three points of confirmation

Sourcing range does not indicate live stock. Availability, price, MOQ and timing are confirmed for the specific part.

01

Define the input

Send part numbers and quantities with a BOM, package, date-code or timing requirements.

02

Check the terms

Check availability, packaging, quotation terms and documents line by line. Present alternatives separately.

03

Confirm and execute

After you approve the quotation and any alternatives, move into picking, verification, packing and dispatch.
