Connecting requirements to delivery.

ZHONGYU XIN supports electronic component procurement through brand sourcing, BOM kitting, supply alternatives and quality verification during order delivery.

Business scope

Component and chip sourcing · BOM kitting · Order delivery

Requirements → Sourcing → Verification → Delivery

Work around each part number

A single part and a multi-line BOM both depend on clear quantities, date codes and timing. We use those requirements to check channels and supply terms, highlight decisions that need your approval, and connect the order to verification and dispatch.

Team structure

How the work moves

Customer contact, purchasing, quality and warehouse roles work from the same requirements. Each handover has its own checks to keep the details together.

Contact and collaboration↗

01Sales and customer contact

Receives inquiries, clarifies requirements, keeps quotation records and follow-up cadence.

02Purchasing and channels

Confirms availability, date codes and pricing; manages suppliers and alternatives.

03Quality

Checks packaging, labels and markings per date code; handles anomalies and return decisions.

04Warehouse and dispatch

Picks, verifies, packs and ships; maintains inbound and outbound records.
